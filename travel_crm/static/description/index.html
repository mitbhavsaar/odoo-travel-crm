🚀 Key Module Features

  • 1. AI Day-Wise Itinerary & Customer Web Portal — Generate complete day-by-day travel plans using AI with a tokenized public web portal (/travel/itinerary/<token>) allowing clients to accept or request modifications directly online.
  • 2. Passport & Visa AI OCR Reader — Auto-extract document details (Passport No, Expiry Date, Nationality, DOB) with instant 6-month validity compliance verification and built-in sample test tools.
  • 3. Smart SLA Lead Auto-Reassignment Engine — Response timers, cron breach detection, and instant lead rotation to available sales representatives with chatter warnings and SLA status badges.
  • 4. Dedicated "Travel Desk" Pipeline — Customized stage workflow (Inquiry ➔ Itinerary Shared ➔ Negotiation ➔ Booking Confirmed ➔ Documentation ➔ Trip Confirmed).
  • 5. AI Lead Intelligence & Summarizer — 1-Click Google Gemini / NVIDIA LLM API integration for automated prospect profiling and next-best-action suggestions.
  • 6. VoIP & Mobile SIM Call Syncing & AI Coaching — Webhook endpoints for call logging, call disposition tracking, and automated sales rep sentiment & skill scoring.
  • 7. Direct WhatsApp Quick-Chat — 1-Click WhatsApp web/app integration directly from the lead record.
  • 8. Payment Installment Tracker & Invoice Wizard — Track partial deposits, milestones, and generate custom Odoo invoices with 1 click.
  • 9. Dynamic Executive Analytics & Exporter — Real-time performance dashboard, Chart.js metrics, and custom multi-step XLSX activity report wizard.
